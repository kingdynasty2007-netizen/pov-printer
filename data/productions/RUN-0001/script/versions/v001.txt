Behind them, the stone walls of Jerusalem catch the warm afternoon light.

=== AUDIO ===
SCENE: scene_001
VOICE: warm male narrator
When David finished speaking to Saul, the soul of Jonathan was knit to the soul of David, and Jonathan loved him as his own soul. In a gesture that would echo through generations, the crown prince stripped off his royal garments and gave them to the man who would one day sit upon the very throne Jonathan was born to inherit.

SCENE: scene_002
VOICE: warm male narrator
Jonathan made a covenant with David because he loved him as himself. He pledged not just friendship, but protection, promising to shield his new brother from the wrath of a king whose jealousy was already taking root.

SCENE: scene_003
VOICE: contemplative male narrator
Yet even as their bond deepened, shadow fell across the palace. Saul's heart grew restless, for the women sang of David—how he had slain thousands, while Saul himself had slain but tens of thousands. The king feared what he could not control.

SCENE: scene_004
VOICE: steady male narrator
Jonathan's gift became David's armor. Wearing the breastplate of the heir apparent, David carried with him not just protection, but a symbol of the covenant between two souls bound by loyalty that transcended throne and lineage.

SCENE: scene_005
VOICE: tense male narrator
In the throne room, Jonathan stood between his father's rage and his friend's fate. He pleaded with Saul to consider the harm David had done to the king, reminding him that David had risked his life for Israel. But pleas fell on deaf ears.

SCENE: scene_006
VOICE: dramatic male narrator
Saul cast the spear—not at Jonathan, but at David. The weapon struck the wall where the young shepherd had stood moments before. Fear gripped the palace, for the king was no longer merely suspicious; he sought David's life.

SCENE: scene_007
VOICE: urgent male narrator
That night, Jonathan slipped away from the palace. He moved through the darkened streets with purpose, carrying news that could save David's life. A son torn between blood and loyalty chose his friend.

SCENE: scene_008
VOICE: tender male narrator
At the gate of Bethlehem, the two friends met once more. They spoke in whispers, knowing the consequences if caught. Jonathan warned David of his father's intent and devised a plan to test the king's resolve.

SCENE: scene_009
VOICE: focused male narrator
Jonathan went out into the field near the stone and shot his arrow as if aiming at a target. Then he called to the boy who served him, saying, Bring the arrow near, seek it out. The signal was simple—a code between friends separated by duty and danger.

SCENE: scene_010
VOICE: concerned male narrator
The young servant ran to retrieve the arrow and told David exactly what he had heard. Jonathan's message was clear: the time had come to flee for his life. The covenant required trust, and trust meant obedience to the warning.

SCENE: scene_011
VOICE: emotional male narrator
They wept together that night, for they knew not when they would meet again. Jonathan renewed his oath to David, swearing that kindness would remain with him always, even if death should separate them. It was a farewell wrapped in promise.

SCENE: scene_012
VOICE: determined male narrator
David rose and departed. He did not look back, for there was no going forward again into the life he had known. He walked toward the wilderness, where caves would become his home and solitude his companion.

SCENE: scene_013
VOICE: sorrowful male narrator
Jonathan stood until David was gone. Alone in the field, he returned to the city with tears hidden behind his eyes. The crown prince who had chosen friendship over throne now faced the wrath of a father who could not understand his loyalty.

SCENE: scene_014
VOICE: heavy male narrator
Saul's house grew darker still. The king who had once been filled with the Spirit now lived in paranoia and rage, his own household turned against him. Jonathan's loyalty to David had become the fracture that split the royal family apart.

SCENE: scene_015
VOICE: reflective male narrator
In the darkness of the wilderness cave, David held Jonathan's sword—a gift that now felt like both a blessing and a burden. The covenant between them remained unbroken, though distance and danger divided them. A faithful friend is stronger than a brother, and their story was far from over.

SCENE: scene_016
VOICE: solemn male narrator
The wilderness stretched before David, vast and uncertain. But even as he fled, the bond forged in loyalty and love endured. What began in the palace courtyard would become a testament to covenant friendship that outlasted kings and empires alike.
