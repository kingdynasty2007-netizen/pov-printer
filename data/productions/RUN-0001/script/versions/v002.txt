=== SCENES ===
CHARACTERS: Saul
Inside the royal palace of Gibeah, King Saul sits on his throne, his face shadowed by a dark turmoil. A spirit of distress weighs heavily upon him. The court is dimly lit by oil lamps, and the king's hands grip the armrests tightly as he stares blankly ahead, troubled and restless.
---
CHARACTERS: David
In a quiet corner of the palace, young David sits holding a wooden harp. His fingers move gently across the strings, playing soothing music. His eyes are closed in concentration, and his expression is calm and peaceful. The warm light of an oil lamp illuminates his gentle face as the melody fills the chamber.
---
CHARACTERS: Saul, David
King Saul sits on his throne as David plays the harp before him. The dark spirit lifts slightly, and Saul's tense shoulders relax. He listens intently, his expression softening as the music brings him moments of relief. Servants stand quietly in the background.
---
CHARACTERS: David, Jonathan
In the palace courtyard, Jonathan, the king's son, first sets eyes on David. His face lights up with wonder and delight. David stands modestly near the harp, while Jonathan approaches him with open arms and a warm smile. Between them, an invisible bond instantly forms — two souls recognizing each other across the stone-paved ground.
---
CHARACTERS: David, Jonathan
Jonathan and David stand face to face in the courtyard, speaking earnestly. Jonathan grips David's hand with both of his own, his expression full of sincerity and love. David looks humbled yet deeply moved. The afternoon sun casts a golden glow around the two young men as their friendship is sealed.
---
CHARACTERS: Jonathan, David
Jonathan takes off his royal robe — the crimson garment marking him as the heir to the throne — and wraps it around David's shoulders. He then unfastens his sword and places it in David's hands. David looks astonished but accepts the gifts with reverence, his eyes glistening. The gesture speaks louder than any words.
---
CHARACTERS: Jonathan, David
Jonathan and David stand together on a stone terrace overlooking the city of Jerusalem. Jonathan places a firm hand on David's shoulder, speaking with deep conviction. David listens with gratitude and awe. The vast landscape stretches behind them — rolling hills and ancient rooftops bathed in warm evening light.
---
CHARACTERS: Saul
In the throne room, King Saul watches from his seat as David stands before the court. The king's face darkens with jealousy and suspicion. His hands clench into fists. The earlier peace is gone, replaced by a dangerous tension that fills the chamber like a gathering storm.
---
CHARACTERS: Saul, Jonathan
Saul hurls a spear toward David, who dodges narrowly. The spear embeds itself in the wall. Jonathan leaps forward, shielding David with his own body, his face contorted with fear and anger. Other guards rush to intervene as the king's rage fills the throne room.
---
CHARACTERS: Jonathan
Jonathan stands alone in a dimly lit corridor of the palace, his face heavy with sorrow. He presses his forehead against the cool stone wall, his hands gripping his sword belt. Outside, thunder rumbles in the distance. He knows his father's heart has turned against his dearest friend, and he is torn between loyalty and love.
---
CHARACTERS: Jonathan
At night, Jonathan slips silently through the palace corridors. He carries a small bundle — his own weapons and provisions — hidden within his cloak. Moonlight streams through narrow windows, casting silver paths on the stone floor. His eyes are determined despite the fear in his heart.
---
CHARACTERS: David, Jonathan
Outside the city gates of Bethlehem, David waits in the shadows of a large stone. Jonathan runs toward him across the dark field, and the two embrace with tearful joy. They speak in urgent whispers, their faces reflecting both the sweetness of reunion and the weight of what lies ahead. Stars shine above them.
---
CHARACTERS: Jonathan
Jonathan stands beside a great stone in an open field near Bethlehem. He nocks an arrow to his bow and draws back with focused intensity. The evening sky is painted in shades of violet and gold. He releases the arrow high into the air, watching it arc upward — a silent signal meant to reach only one person.
---
CHARACTERS: David
A young servant boy — Jonathan's trusted attendant — runs across the field toward David, who stands waiting near a pile of stones. The servant points urgently toward the sky where Jonathan's arrow has landed. David's face shifts from hope to solemn understanding, knowing now that he must flee for his life.
---
CHARACTERS: Jonathan, David
David and Jonathan stand together one final time beside the great stone in the field. They embrace deeply, both men weeping openly. Jonathan whispers words of covenant and love, promising that goodness will pursue David always. David clings to his friend, knowing this may be their last meeting. The sky darkens around them.
