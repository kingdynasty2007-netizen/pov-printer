=== SCENES ===
CHARACTERS: Jesus, Lawyer
Jesus stands in a dusty courtyard in Jerusalem surrounded by a growing crowd. Before him a devout lawyer steps forward, his face filled with a searching earnestness. He poses a question that echoes through the gathered crowd.
---
CHARACTERS: Lawyer
The lawyer stands alone against the golden afternoon light of Jerusalem, his brow furrowed with deep conviction. His lips move as he speaks a weighty question about the requirements of eternal life.
---
CHARACTERS: Jesus
Jesus looks directly at the lawyer with calm penetrating wisdom, responding not with a simple answer but with a question of His own, turning the conversation back to the Scriptures.
---
CHARACTERS: Samaritan, Traveler
A wide shot of the dangerous road from Jerusalem down to Jericho, a desolate winding path through barren rocky hills. In the distance a lone traveler walks with bags and supplies, unaware of the danger ahead.
---
CHARACTERS: Traveler
The traveler lies on the dusty roadside, stripped of his belongings, beaten and half-conscious. Scattered coins and torn garments surround him as shadows of departing attackers fade over the ridge.
---
CHARACTERS: Priest
A priest in white linen garments walks along the same road, his face set in solemn duty. He spots the wounded man, pauses only briefly, then turns sharply and crosses to the other side of the path, walking away.
---
CHARACTERS: Levite
A Levite approaches the scene, his ceremonial garments marking him as a temple servant. He too glances at the suffering traveler, hesitates, and then continues on without stopping.
---
CHARACTERS: Samaritan
A Samaritan traveler rounds a rocky bend and sees the wounded man. His face transforms from weary travel into deep compassion. He immediately drops his packs and runs toward the stranger.
---
CHARACTERS: Samaritan, Traveler
The Samaritan kneels beside the beaten traveler, gently cleaning his wounds with water from a skin. He wraps strips of cloth around the man's injuries with careful tenderness, speaking words of comfort.
---
CHARACTERS: Samaritan, Traveler
The Samaritan helps the injured man onto his sturdy donkey and mounts behind him, supporting the traveler's head against his shoulder as they begin the journey onward through the rugged terrain.
---
CHARACTERS: Samaritan
At a quiet stone inn nestled among olive trees, the Samaritan carries the traveler inside. The innkeeper opens the door as the Samaritan lays the man gently upon a sleeping mat.
---
CHARACTERS: Samaritan
The next morning the Samaritan places two silver coins into the innkeeper's hand. He kneels beside the traveler one more time, speaking words of farewell and entrusting the man to the innkeeper's care.
---
CHARACTERS: Jesus, Lawyer
Jesus stands before the assembled crowd and the lawyer, His voice carrying with divine authority. He asks the lawyer which of the three men proved to be a neighbor to the wounded traveler.
---
CHARACTERS: Jesus, Lawyer
The lawyer lowers his eyes, unable to name the Samaritan directly, and murmurs the answer. Jesus looks upon him with loving gravity and issues the final command that closes the parable.
---
CHARACTERS: Jesus
Jesus faces the crowd with open arms and a compassionate expression, His words of the parable settling over the listeners like dawn light, challenging each heart to recognize who their neighbor truly is.
---
CHARACTERS: jesus, lawyer
Jesus looks at the lawyer with gentleness, His expression full of compassion rather than triumph. The lawyer stands humbled, his proud posture softened, his eyes lowered as he carries the weight of his own answer. Around them, the crowd falls into a deep, thoughtful silence. Reference: Luke 10:37
---
CHARACTERS: jesus, traveler, samaritan
Jesus raises His hand and paints the scene again in the minds of the listeners, gesturing as if showing them the dusty road once more. Behind Him, a visual echo appears — the injured traveler lying on the ground, the Samaritan bending over him with tender care. The priest and the Levite fade into shadow, forgotten figures passing by. Reference: Luke 10:33-34
---
CHARACTERS: jesus
Jesus turns fully toward the crowd, His arms open wide, His voice ringing with quiet power. The golden light of late afternoon wraps around Him like a promise. The camera pulls back slowly, showing the entire gathering still, each person carrying the question within themselves — Who will I choose to be my neighbor? Reference: Luke 10:37

=== AUDIO ===
SCENE: scene_001
VOICE: Sage
There was a man who came to Jesus with a question that had been weighing on his heart for a long time.

SCENE: scene_002
VOICE: Sage
With sincere earnestness, the Jewish lawyer asked, "Teacher, what must I do to inherit eternal life?"

SCENE: scene_003
VOICE: Sage
Jesus, in His heavenly wisdom, did not give a direct answer. Instead He turned the question back, asking what was written in the Law of Moses.

SCENE: scene_004
VOICE: Sage
The lawyer replied correctly, quoting the Scriptures: love God with all your heart, and love your neighbor as yourself.

SCENE: scene_005
VOICE: Sage
But still the man wanted to justify himself, so he asked, "And who exactly is my neighbor?" Then Jesus told them a story, a story that would change hearts forever.

SCENE: scene_006
VOICE: Narrator
A man was traveling down the terrible road from Jerusalem to Jericho, a road known for its bandits and danger.

SCENE: scene_007
VOICE: Narrator
Robbers fell upon him, stripped him of everything, beat him cruelly, and left him half-dead upon the ground, bleeding and alone.

SCENE: scene_008
VOICE: Narrator
By divine providence, a priest happened to be traveling that same road. When he saw the wounded man, he looked at him, but then crossed to the other side and passed by on the far side.

SCENE: scene_009
VOICE: Narrator
Likewise, a Levite came to the place, saw the man, and also passed by on the other side, leaving the suffering traveler in his distress.

SCENE: scene_010
VOICE: Narrator
But a Samaritan, traveling along that very path, came upon the wounded man. When he saw him, he was overcome with pity.

SCENE: scene_011
VOICE: Narrator
The Samaritan went to him, bound up his wounds, pouring on oil and wine as a remedy. He gave the man water and tended to him with gentle care.

SCENE: scene_012
VOICE: Narrator
He lifted the broken man onto his own donkey and brought him to an inn, where he cared for him through the night, never leaving his side.

SCENE: scene_013
VOICE: Narrator
The next morning, the Samaritan took out two denarii, gave them to the innkeeper, and said, "Take care of him, and whatever more you spend, I will repay you when I return."

SCENE: scene_014
VOICE: Sage
Then Jesus asked the lawyer, "Which of these three do you think proved to be a neighbor to the man who fell among the robbers?"

SCENE: scene_015
VOICE: Sage
The lawyer could only whisper, "The one who showed mercy toward him." And Jesus said to him, "Go and do likewise, and you too shall live."

SCENE: scene_016
VOICE: warm_narrator
The lawyer could say nothing more. His clever questions had led him to his own answer, and there was no escaping it. Jesus looked at him not with judgment, but with mercy — the same mercy the Samaritan had shown the broken traveler. The crowd stood in silence, each heart stirred by the truth that had been spoken.

SCENE: scene_017
VOICE: warm_narrator
Jesus painted the scene once more in their minds. The priest passed by. The Levite passed by. But the Samaritan — the one they had counted as an outsider — saw the man, felt compassion, and gave everything he had to help. The image of wounded hands being bound, of oil and wine poured upon scars, lingered like a living lesson.

SCENE: scene_018
VOICE: warm_narrator
And so Jesus opened His arms to all who heard, and the words fell upon them like seeds in good soil. Go and do likewise, He said. The question was no longer who is my neighbor, but whose neighbor will I be?
