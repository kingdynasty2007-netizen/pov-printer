=== AUDIO ===
SCENE: scene_001
VOICE: calm narrator
The valley below was silent except for the wind. But on the horizon, fire flickered — the Philistine army had gathered once more. And among them stood a warrior so terrible that not a single Israelite soldier dared face him.

SCENE: scene_002
VOICE: calm narrator
Yet God had not chosen a soldier. He had sent the prophet Samuel to the house of Jesse in Bethlehem, to anoint a king — not from among the mighty, but from the least known son.

SCENE: scene_003
VOICE: calm narrator
The oil poured like a promise. In that quiet moment, the youngest shepherd boy became the future ruler of Israel. God had seen what no one else could.

SCENE: scene_004
VOICE: calm narrator
David tended his father's sheep in the hills above Bethlehem. He played his harp, sang praises, and learned patience under the open sky. Little did he know, those quiet years would prepare him for the greatest battle of all.

SCENE: scene_005
VOICE: calm narrator
When an evil spirit tormented King Saul, his servants found a solution. They brought David from the pasture, and his music became a shield against despair. The boy who played for sheep now played for a troubled king.

SCENE: scene_006
VOICE: calm narrator
But even kings could not protect Israel forever. The people needed a warrior. And David, though still young, felt the call of duty that would change the fate of a nation.

SCENE: scene_007
VOICE: calm narrator
Before he could face the giant, David learned to trust. He struck down lions and bears with nothing but a staff and a stone. He chose five smooth stones from a stream, knowing God would guide his aim.

SCENE: scene_008
VOICE: calm narrator
David left the safety of the camp and walked toward the battle alone. No armor, no sword borrowed from another — only his faith and the weapons of a shepherd. The Israelites watched in stunned silence.

SCENE: scene_009
VOICE: calm narrator
King Saul offered David his own royal armor, but it was too heavy for the young man. David refused. This was not a battle of swords and shields — it was a battle of faith.

SCENE: scene_010
VOICE: calm narrator
The giant laughed at the boy. He mocked Israel's God and cursed David by his gods. But David answered with words that shook the valley: 'You come against me with sword and spear, but I come in the name of the Lord.'

SCENE: scene_011
VOICE: calm narrator
David's hand moved with precision. The stone left the sling and flew through the air like lightning. It struck the giant where no armor could protect him, and the mighty warrior fell face first into the dust.

SCENE: scene_012
VOICE: calm narrator
The Philistines turned and ran. Israel rose and pursued them across the valley. And the boy from Bethlehem, once unknown, became the strongest man in Israel — not because of his size, but because of his faith.
