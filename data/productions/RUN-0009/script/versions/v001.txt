=== AUDIO ===
SCENE: scene_001
VOICE: warm elderly male narrator
A father stood at the edge of his courtyard, his eyes fixed on the dusty road that wound away toward the distant land. He had waited many days. He had waited many nights. And still, his younger son did not return.

SCENE: scene_002
VOICE: warm elderly male narrator
It had begun with a reckless demand. The younger son had come to his father and said, "Father, give me the share of property that falls to me." It was as if he were already dead. The father divided his wealth between his two boys and sent them on their way.

SCENE: scene_003
VOICE: warm elderly male narrator
The younger son sold everything and packed his bags. He traveled far from home, to a country that knew nothing of his father's house. There he threw himself into wild living, spending his money on pleasures that left him emptier with each passing day.

SCENE: scene_004
VOICE: warm elderly male narrator
But when he had spent it all, a terrible famine struck that land. Every coin was gone, and no one gave him anything. The hunger grew so fierce that he hired himself out to a local farmer, feeding pigs — animals his people considered unclean.

SCENE: scene_005
VOICE: warm elderly male narrator
He longed to eat the pods the swine were eating, but no one gave him any. In his desperation, he came to his senses. "How many of my father's hired servants have food to spare," he thought, "and here I am, starving to death."

SCENE: scene_006
VOICE: warm elderly male narrator
"I will go back to him and say, 'Father, I have sinned against heaven and against you. I am no longer worthy to be called your son.'" So he began the long journey home, his feet sore, his heart heavy with shame.

SCENE: scene_007
VOICE: warm elderly male narrator
But while he was still far off, his father saw him. The old man ran — not walked, not hurried, but ran — and threw his arms around his son's neck and kissed him.

SCENE: scene_008
VOICE: warm elderly male narrator
"Son," the father said, his voice trembling with joy, "you are my son. Come, bring forth the best robe, put a ring on your hand, and kill the fattened calf. Let us celebrate."

SCENE: scene_009
VOICE: warm elderly male narrator
And so the lost boy was found. The dead was alive again. And in that house, music and dancing filled the air as a father's love welcomed home a son who had wandered too far.

SCENE: scene_010
VOICE: warm elderly male narrator
But outside, the older son stood in the shadow of the doorway, his face hard with resentment. He had stayed faithful, yet here his brother was celebrated for simply returning.

SCENE: scene_011
VOICE: warm elderly male narrator
The father went out to him, pleading with his hands open. "Son," he said, "you are always with me, and all that is mine is yours. But we had to celebrate, for your brother was dead, and is alive again."

SCENE: scene_012
VOICE: warm elderly male narrator
The older son looked at his father, then at his brother, and slowly, his hardened expression began to soften. The feast continued within, a song of redemption echoing into the night.

SCENE: scene_013
VOICE: warm elderly male narrator
For a father's love does not keep score. It does not count the cost. It simply runs to meet the wandering child, no matter how far they have strayed.

SCENE: scene_014
VOICE: warm elderly male narrator
And in that house, two brothers stood together — one who had been lost and found, and one who had never left but needed to remember: grace is not earned, it is given.

SCENE: scene_015
VOICE: warm elderly male narrator
The road home is long, but the father's arms are always open. For where there is repentance, there is celebration. Where there is return, there is restoration. This is the story of the prodigal son.

SCENE: scene_016
VOICE: warm male narrator
The older son could not hide his bitterness. "All these years I have served you," he said, "and never once did you give me a young goat to celebrate with my friends. But when this son of yours returns after swallowing your wealth with prostitutes, you kill the fattened calf for him!" Reference: Luke 15:29-30

The father did not rebuke him. Instead he drew near and spoke with gentle urgency. "My son, you are always with me. Everything I have is yours." Reference: Luke 15:31

"But we had to celebrate and be glad, for your brother was dead and is alive again. He was lost, and now he is found." Reference: Luke 15:32

SCENE: scene_017
VOICE: warm male narrator
The younger son stepped into the light, his body still bearing the marks of his long suffering. He had returned with nothing but a broken heart and a repentant spirit. Yet here he stood, alive, embraced by the father who ran to meet him.

Now he watched his older brother, afraid that anger would harden again. The father kept his arm around the elder son, pulling him close, refusing to let resentment build a wall between them.

SCENE: scene_018
VOICE: warm male narrator
Then something shifted. The older son uncrossed his arms. His hand rose slowly and rested upon his brother's shoulder — heavy, uncertain, but real. The younger son looked up through tear-filled eyes and nodded, understanding everything that had been passed between them without a single word.

Two brothers stood together at last. Not because the past was forgotten, but because grace was greater than the pain. The father watched them, and in his heart, the celebration grew even deeper — for now both sons were found.
